RESEARCH

Research Objectives & Findings

MICROPLASTICS + PFAS

Combined environmental exposures

Objective: Examine how PFAS and microplastics interact and how these interactions influence environmental behavior and exposure.

Findings: Sorption and co-exposure depend on polymer characteristics, PFAS chemistry, environmental conditions, aging, and biological context.

HARMFUL ALGAL BLOOMS

Environmental drivers and health impacts

Objective: Evaluate drivers, impacts, monitoring methods, and management strategies.

Findings: Nutrient enrichment, warming, hydrologic conditions, and ecosystem change contribute to bloom risk and associated health concerns.

PLASTIC POLLUTION

Risk across aquatic systems

Objective: Understand persistence, transport, exposure, and risk from plastic pollution.

Findings: Particle size, polymer composition, aging, additives, and contaminant interactions influence environmental and biological behavior.

NANOTECHNOLOGY & DRUG DELIVERY

Toxicity and transformation

Objective: Review how nanotechnology-based drug delivery systems can improve the delivery of therapeutic agents, including their ability to enhance targeting, control drug release, and improve treatment effectiveness.

Findings: Nanotechnology-based delivery systems can improve drug stability, bioavailability, and targeted delivery while potentially reducing unwanted systemic effects. Their effectiveness depends on factors such as nanoparticle composition, size, surface properties, drug-loading capacity, and release behavior.
